## What Makes SOMERA Special?

### 1. Built on Shweta's Coaching Framework
SOMERA is trained on Shweta's unique 4-Step Framework and coaching methodology — not generic AI responses. It mirrors her coaching style, language, and approach.

### 2. Emotional Pattern Recognition
When a visitor shares what's bothering them, SOMERA recognizes the emotional patterns and helps them uncover the root cause — just like a skilled coach would.

### 3. The Three Pillars
SOMERA understands the interconnection between **Career**, **Relationships**, and **Wellness** — gently guiding conversations across these life areas.

### 4. Active Listening, Not Advice-Giving
SOMERA doesn't tell people what to do. Instead, it asks the right questions, reflects back what it hears, and helps users arrive at their own clarity.

### 5. Smart Referrals
When someone needs deeper support (energy healing, chakra work, regression therapy, etc.), SOMERA gracefully guides them toward booking a Discovery Call with Shweta.
